Privacy Policy & Data Handling

Last updated: 17 October 2026

🔐 Core Promise: Zero Data Transmission

Data processed entirely client-side in the browser.
Zero operational logs. Zero external server transmission. Your fleet IMOs, voyage details, bunker delivery notes, and consumption figures never leave your device.

How CarbonKeel Works

  • Public Lookup (Free Search): When you search for a ship by IMO or name, CarbonKeel queries THETIS-MRV public register data. This is publicly available EU compliance data. Your search itself is logged locally in your browser history only.
  • Owner Data Input (Calculator): When you enter your vessel's DWT, fuel consumption, voyage legs, or fleet details, all calculations run in your browser. Your keyboard input never leaves your device.
  • No Cookies for Tracking: CarbonKeel uses browser localStorage only to remember your access key and recent searches. No third-party analytics. No Google/Facebook pixels. No email harvesting.

Data You Provide: What We Do

  • Search Queries (IMO/Name): Processed locally. Not logged server-side.
  • Fleet Data (DWT, fuel, speed, voyage splits): Processed in your browser. Not sent to our servers. Not stored unless you explicitly export as PDF.
  • Email (Contact Form): Only sent if you submit the contact form. Used to reply to your question. Not shared, not sold, not retained beyond reply.
  • Access Keys: Your key is stored locally in browser. Not transmitted except to validate against our list (which lives in site code).

GDPR & Commercial Confidentiality

  • CarbonKeel is not a data processor. You are the sole data controller.
  • No personal data is collected (no IP logging, no location tracking, no user profiling).
  • No BDNs (Bunker Delivery Notes), consumption logs, or off-hire data is ever transmitted.
  • You own all calculation results. Export as PDF if needed for board presentations or bank refinancing.

External Data Sources

  • THETIS-MRV: EU public register, accessed via read-only API. EMSA publishes this under Article 21, Regulation (EU) 2015/757. Attribution and disclaimer in footer of every page.
  • Third-Party Regulatory References: Regulation texts linked (EUR-Lex, IMO, MEPC documents). These are external; we are not liable for their uptime or changes.

Security & Liability

Not Legal or Financial Advice.
CarbonKeel estimates are labelled as such and rebuild from public data. They are cross-checks, not official verifier figures. Do not rely on CarbonKeel for compliance filings, contractual disputes, or financial instruments without independent verification.

Your Rights

  • Right to Export: Download your calculations as PDF anytime.
  • Right to Delete: Clear your browser localStorage to delete stored keys and search history.
  • Right to Access: All your data is in your browser; you own it.
  • Right to Opt-Out: Stop using CarbonKeel anytime. No account to delete (there is no account).
