Estimate from speed from a known consumption point, main engine and auxiliaries apart

Main-engine consumption per day scales with (speed ÷ reference speed)n. n = 3 is the cube law; ships with an engine or shaft power limitation, or a modern hull, often sit between about 2.6 and 3.5, so use the exponent from your own speed-consumption curve. Auxiliary, boiler, reefer and hotel load is entered separately per day and does not scale with speed. Distance is speed × 24 × days at sea unless you enter a logged distance.

3 Where was the fuel burned? (for the EU ETS, UK ETS and FuelEU bill)

Share of the year’s fuel, by mass. Each share is charged by its own rule: voyages between EU/EEA ports and EU berths at 100%, voyages into or out of the EU/EEA at 50%, UK domestic voyages and UK berths at 100% of the UK scheme, Great Britain to Northern Ireland at 50%. The rest is out of scope.

Your data: where it comes from and how to control it

The verified figures we show are published by EMSA because Article 21 of the EU MRV Regulation requires it. Figures we derive from them are our own estimates, and you can switch those off.

Is this your ship? Take control

Claim it, correct our figures, or have our estimates hidden from third parties. Hiding takes effect straight away, and we confirm by email once it is permanent.
